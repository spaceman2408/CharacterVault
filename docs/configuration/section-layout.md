# Section Tab Layout

The workspace tab strip at the top of the character editor has **18 section tabs** by default — most of which you'll use regularly, but some you might never touch. The **Sections** tab in Settings lets you hide the tabs you don't need and reorder the rest however you like.

::: tip
Your tab layout is stored locally in IndexedDB. It's not synced anywhere — it lives with the rest of your settings on this device.
:::

## Accessing Tab Layout Settings

1. Open a character in the workspace.
2. Click **Settings** in the workspace header.
3. Click the **Sections** tab.
4. Make your changes, then click **Save Settings**.

The tab strip updates as soon as you save. On mobile, the dropdown also reflects the new order and hides the same sections.

## Showing and Hiding Tabs

Each section is listed with its label and a short description. Click the **👁 eye** icon on the right to hide a tab.

Hidden sections move below a **"Hidden"** divider. To bring a tab back, click the **👁‍🗨 eye-off** icon next to it.

::: tip
You can hide every tab if you really want to — the editor still works because the last-active section stays in place. You can always re-enable tabs from Settings.
:::

## Reordering Tabs

Each visible tab has **▲** and **▼** arrow buttons on the left. Click them to move the tab up or down by one position.

The arrow buttons swap the tab with the *adjacent visible tab*, so you can move a tab right next to another one regardless of what's hidden in between.

Disabled arrow buttons (at 25% opacity) tell you when you're at the top or bottom of the visible list.

## Reset to Defaults

Click **Reset to Defaults** at the top of the tab to restore the original section order and show every section. This is a local change — it only takes effect after you click **Save Settings**.

## New Sections

When a new section type is added in a future update, it appears at the end of the visible list automatically. You don't need to manually enable it.
